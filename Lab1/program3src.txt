Sample text refers to placeholder content used to demonstrate or preview design, format, or functionality before the final content is added. It is often generic and lacks significant meaning, serving to test layout, readability, and user experience without the distraction of meaningful content.

 The text is used in graphic design, publishing, and web development to allow page layouts to be designed independently of the final copy.
